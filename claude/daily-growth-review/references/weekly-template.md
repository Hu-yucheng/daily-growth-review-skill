## Synthesis rules

- Do not rewrite seven diaries.
- Prefer closure, recurrence reduction, comparable change, and transfer evidence over insight volume.
- Distinguish `missed` from `blocked`.
- Update the private profile only when roles, goals, constraints, resources, or boundaries materially changed.
